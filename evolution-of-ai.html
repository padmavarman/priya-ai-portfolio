Evolution of AI: Six Decades of Innovation

A team presentation with Group 3, tracing the history of artificial intelligence from its theoretical origins to today's large language models.

About This Artifact

As part of our AI/ML coursework, my team and I researched and built a presentation tracing the evolution of artificial intelligence, from Alan Turing's 1950 proposal of the Turing Test all the way to the large language models shaping the field today. Each milestone in the deck is backed by a cited primary or academic source, from original research papers to company announcements.

The timeline covers six major eras:

  • 1950–1956: The Turing Test and the founding of AI as an academic field at the Dartmouth Conference
  • 1956–1972: Early milestones like ELIZA, the first chatbot, and Shakey, an early AI-controlled robot
  • 1970–1990: Expert systems like MYCIN, followed by funding cuts during the first "AI winter"
  • 1990–2004: Landmark wins including IBM's Deep Blue and early consumer AI like Roomba and Sony's AIBO
  • 2005–2012: The road to deep learning, self-driving breakthroughs, ImageNet, GPUs, and AlexNet
  • 2013–2019: Transformers, GANs, GPT, BERT, and the foundations of modern generative AI
