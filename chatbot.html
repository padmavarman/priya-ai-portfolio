DSO Daddy: An AI Agent for International Student Support

A team project with Group 3, built in the AI Lab, an AI agent that answers DSO-related questions for international students at Indiana Wesleyan University.

Introduction Description Objective Process Tools Value Proposition
Try DSO Daddy

Introduction

International students juggle a lot of confusing, high-stakes paperwork, CPT, OPT, H-1B, F-1 status, Green Cards, often with limited, hard-to-schedule access to their school's international student office (DSO). My team and I built DSO Daddy to close that gap: an AI agent that gives IWU students quick, grounded answers to DSO-related questions whenever they need them.

Description

This artifact is a live, deployed AI chatbot built for Group 3's AI Lab project. DSO Daddy answers common international student questions, starting with F-1 student visa topics before expanding toward work-visa topics like OPT and CPT, and was trained/grounded using information pulled directly from Indiana Wesleyan University's own website so its answers stay specific to IWU's actual policies and resources.

Objective

The goal was twofold: build something that genuinely reduces the stress international students feel navigating visa rules, and, for me personally, learn what it actually takes to go from an idea to a working AI product, not just discuss AI/ML concepts in theory, but evaluate real tools, make build decisions, and ship something usable.

Process

We started in an idea phase, identifying DSO-related confusion as a problem worth solving since several of us on the team had personally run into it. From there we moved into a platform-testing phase, running the same prompts through ChatGPT, Claude, and Copilot to see how each handled sensitive, specific immigration questions, and used those tests to work through how the chatbot should be prompted and where a legal disclaimer was necessary.

In the build phase, we trialed two no-code AI app builders, Lovable and Chatbase, before settling on Lovable. We told Lovable the agent was being built for Indiana Wesleyan University specifically, and it pulled information directly from IWU's own website to ground DSO Daddy's answers, rather than relying on generic, unverified information.

Tools and Technologies Used

  • ChatGPT, Claude, and Copilot — used to test and compare prompt handling before building
  • Lovable — the no-code AI app builder used to build and deploy the final chatbot
  • Chatbase — evaluated as an alternative build platform during testing
  • Indiana Wesleyan University's website — used as the grounding source for DSO Daddy's answers

Relevance

What DSO Daddy Can Help With

  • Travel documents and signatures needed for OPT
  • When F-1 students can enter the U.S. before their program start date
  • How an F-1 transfer between SEVP-certified schools works
  • General DSO responsibilities, like issuing the I-20, advising on status rules, and approving work authorization
DSO Daddy is not a substitute for legal advice. Students are always encouraged to confirm answers with their actual DSO or an immigration attorney.
