DSO Daddy: An AI Agent for International Student Support

A team project with Group 3, built in the AI Lab, an AI agent that answers DSO-related questions for international students at Indiana Wesleyan University.

Try DSO Daddy

About This Artifact

My team and I built DSO Daddy after realizing how many of us had personally dealt with the confusion of navigating international student visa questions. We wanted to create something that could help other students get quick, reliable answers about DSO-related topics like CPT, OPT, H-1B, F-1 visas, and Green Cards, starting with student visa questions before expanding into work visa topics.

We tested several AI platforms, including ChatGPT, Claude, and Copilot, comparing how each handled the same prompts before settling on Lovable to actually build the app. We told Lovable we were building this for Indiana Wesleyan University, and it pulled information directly from the university's own websites to ground DSO Daddy's answers.

What DSO Daddy Can Help With

  • Travel documents and signatures needed for OPT
  • When F-1 students can enter the U.S. before their program start date
  • How an F-1 transfer between SEVP-certified schools works
  • General DSO responsibilities, like issuing the I-20, advising on status rules, and approving work authorization
DSO Daddy is not a substitute for legal advice. Students are always encouraged to confirm answers with their actual DSO or an immigration attorney.
